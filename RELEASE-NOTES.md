# Thermal Treatment V1

V1 supports validated repeating within-wall continuous Z-girt and rail constructions for design decision support.

It is not compliance certification, engineering sign-off, or a manufacturer-specific system assessment. It does not support arbitrary three-dimensional fixings, clips, screws, anchors, junctions, or manufacturer-specific systems.

Results remain **Preliminary calculations - not verified** until an architect confirms the required fabrication and boundary-condition inputs. Only confirmed inputs within the family validation envelope and a passing compatible worker produce **Verified results**.

The report retains the layer-only U-value beside the effective U-value and preserves family, pack, worker, convergence, input, and solver-artifact evidence.
